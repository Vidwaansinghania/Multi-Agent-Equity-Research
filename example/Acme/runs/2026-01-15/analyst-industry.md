# Industry and competition — ACME

Every figure below is invented. See `run.md`.

## Where the company sits

Acme is the third-largest supplier of industrial coatings in North America by
revenue, behind Northbridge Chemical and Calder Specialty, and the second-largest
in specialty adhesives. Coatings is a commodity business with a service layer;
adhesives is formulation work with switching costs measured in requalification
time.

## Porter's five forces

| Force | Strength | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Rivalry | High in coatings, moderate in adhesives | Four suppliers hold 71% of the coatings market and price on annual contracts; adhesives contracts run three to five years with requalification of six to nine months |
| Buyer power | High | The three largest coatings customers are 21% of segment revenue and negotiate annually |
| Supplier power | Moderate | Two resin inputs are 34% of cost of goods and both have three or more suppliers |
| New entrants | Low | A coatings line costs roughly $180m and takes two years to permit |
| Substitutes | Low in adhesives, moderate in coatings | Mechanical fastening substitutes for adhesives at a weight penalty; powder coatings substitute for liquid in some applications |

## Unit economics against peers

| Company | Operating margin | ROIC | Revenue per employee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acme Materials | 19.3% | 16.4% | $612k |
| Northbridge Chemical | 16.1% | 12.8% | $548k |
| Calder Specialty | 21.4% | 18.2% | $703k |
| Ferro-Lane Industries | 12.7% | 9.4% | $471k |

Acme sits between Northbridge and Calder on every measure, which is consistent
with a mix that is two thirds commodity and one third formulation. Calder's
premium is a purer adhesives book.

## Structural change

Two things are moving. Regulation on volatile organic compounds tightens in three
states in 2027 and favours the water-based formulations Acme already sells, worth
perhaps 1% of coatings revenue at a better margin. And two large coatings
customers have publicly said they are evaluating insourcing, which is the
specific risk the bear case is built on.
