## What changed in the filings

The Q3 fiscal 2025 8-K on 2025-10-29 cut FY2026 revenue growth guidance to 5%
from the 6% given in July, and named two causes: slower order intake from two
large coatings customers, and a delayed ramp at the Dalton adhesives plant. Gross
margin guidance was unchanged.

The FY2025 10-K, filed 2026-02-18, added a risk factor on customer
concentration that was not in the FY2024 filing. The three largest coatings
customers were 21% of segment revenue in FY2025, up from 17% in FY2023.
