# Bear case — ACME

Every figure below is invented. See `run.md`.

The base case is too generous, and the reason is concentration the filings only
started disclosing in the most recent year.

## The customer concentration is new and moving fast

The FY2025 10-K added a risk factor the FY2024 filing did not have. The three
largest coatings customers went from 17% of segment revenue in FY2023 to 21% in
FY2025, and two of them have publicly said they are evaluating insourcing. On the
segment's $5,510m of FY2025 revenue, 21% is $1,157m, and two of the three is
roughly $600m, or 7.1% of total revenue at a segment margin the fundamentals file
puts near the corporate average.

Losing that over two years takes FY2028 revenue to $8,900m instead of $9,900m and
FY2028 EPS to about $4.95. On the base case's own 20.5x that is a $101 stock, and
the multiple would not stay at 20.5x through a disclosed customer loss.

## The margin forecast ignores what the guidance cut said

The October 8-K named slower order intake from those same two customers as a
cause of the revenue cut. The valuation memo takes the revenue cut and holds
margin at 19.6%, 30bp above FY2025. Coatings is a fixed-cost business on the
industry analyst's own account, a $180m line and a two-year permit, so volume
that does not arrive de-levers the plant. Margin at 18.4% by FY2028, which is the
FY2022 level rather than a trough, takes free cash flow down about 6% and the
value per share to $96.70.

## The reverse case is where the memo's own number points

The memo's reverse discounted cash flow says the market implies 2.05% terminal
growth against 2.50% assumed. The memo reads that as a narrow disagreement. Read
the other way, a 45bp gap on terminal growth against a name with 21% of a
segment's revenue in three hands and two of them evaluating exit is the market
pricing the disclosed risk while the model does not.

## Arithmetic check on the memo

Two items. The terminal reinvestment rate is given as 0.179 against 0.025 / 0.14
= 0.1786, which rounds correctly and reconciles. And the sensitivity grid's 20.5x
row reads $105.6, $111.7, $117.9 against 20.5 x $5.45 = $111.7, which also
reconciles. No error found.

## What would change my mind

A Q1 or Q2 fiscal 2026 print showing coatings volume up year on year with the
concentration figure flat or lower, or either named customer signing a multi-year
renewal. On a renewal I would move to the base case as written.
