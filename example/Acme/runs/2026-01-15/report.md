# Acme Materials Corporation — HOLD, $108.50 target

Acme Materials Corporation is invented and every figure in this report is a
fixture. The report exists to show what the export scripts produce.

## 01 Company overview

Acme Materials Corporation makes industrial coatings and specialty adhesives for
manufacturers, and sells almost entirely business to business in North America.
Fiscal 2025 revenue was $8,400m, up 5.3%, with industrial coatings at $5,510m and
specialty adhesives at $2,890m. Coatings is 65.6% of revenue and the lower-margin
half; adhesives carries the formulation work and the longer contracts.

The company runs eleven plants, employs about 13,700 people and has raised its
dividend in each of the past nine years. Fiscal years end December 31. Revenue per
employee was $612k in FY2025, between Northbridge Chemical at $548k and Calder
Specialty at $703k.

Revenue grew at a 5.5% compound rate over the ten years to FY2025, with a single
down year in FY2020. Operating margin widened from 16.1% to 19.3% over the same
span, and gross margin did 1.8 points of that while SG&A held flat as a share of
revenue.

## 02 Investment thesis and variant perception

The market treats Acme as a cyclical coatings supplier trading slightly above its
own history at 19.7x forward earnings against a ten-year median of 18.2x. On that
reading the stock is fully priced.

Two facts sit against it. Capital deployed in FY2025 earned a 20.5% incremental
return against a 15.1% ten-year average, so new money is earning more than the
existing base, which is the reverse of the normal pattern in a maturing chemicals
book. And free cash flow conversion has averaged 108% of net income for a decade
without falling below 92%, so the reported margin expansion is cash rather than
accrual.

The variant perception is narrow and it is on the risk rather than the quality.
Three coatings customers are 21% of segment revenue, up from 17% two years ago,
and two of them have said publicly they are evaluating insourcing. The market is
pricing about 45bp less terminal growth than this valuation assumes, which is not
enough to be pricing that risk. Whether it should be is the question this call
turns on, and the answer arrives with the Q1 print on 2026-04-23.

The conclusion is a hold rather than a disagreement. The business is better than
the multiple implies by roughly $8 a share, and the concentration risk is worth
roughly $36 a share at a 25% probability. Those cancel inside the band.

adhesives. Four suppliers hold 71% of the coatings market.

## 08 Bull, base, bear and recommendation

The bull case takes operating margin to 21.0% by FY2029 on the ten-year trend and
gets $138 at 24.0x FY2027E earnings per share of $5.75. The bear case has two
coatings customers insourcing over two years, cutting FY2028 revenue by about
$1,000m, and gets $72 at 14.0x on $5.15.

HOLD, target $108.50, expected total return of +10.7% including $2.20 of
dividends. The call is 4.3 points inside the band edge and one input moves it: the
bear probability. At 15% bear with 35% base the target is $117.15 and the expected
return is +19.4%, which is a BUY. A multi-year renewal from either named customer
is the event that would justify that change.
