# Risk gate — ACME

Every figure below is invented. See `run.md`. The rating is not this agent's to
change.

## The existing position

None. The snapshot dated 2026-01-12 shows no ACME. Every figure below is
therefore about a position that would be opened rather than one held.

## What a position would do to the book

A 3% position at $100 in a portfolio of this size is 4.1% of the equity sleeve.
That is inside the 5% single-name limit and it would become the fourth-largest
holding. Nothing in the book is currently in specialty chemicals, so the
concentration cost is sector rather than name.

## Correlation with what is already held

Two overlaps worth naming. The industrials holding moves with the same industrial
production series that drives Acme's coatings volume, and the two would fall
together in a manufacturing slowdown. The building products holding shares the
housing input on roughly a third of its revenue, and Acme's coatings book has no
housing exposure, so that overlap is weaker than it looks.

## Round-trip cost

The account is funded in CAD and Acme trades in USD. Trades are commission-free
but conversion lands about 1.9% off mid in each direction, so the round trip costs
about 3.8%.

Against an expected total return of +10.7% that leaves +6.9% net, which still
justifies the trade if the thesis holds. This is the arithmetic that matters on a
marginal call: at an expected return of +4% the currency cost would take more than
half the edge, and at +3.8% it would take all of it.
