One row per company, newest call only. Every field is copied from that company's
newest `decision.md` front-matter and never computed here. Update the row in stage 7,
after the calls-log row is written.

| Company | Ticker | Latest call | Target | Latest run | Prior coverage call | Diverged |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [<Company>](<Company>/_index.md) | <TICKER> | <BUY / HOLD / SELL> | $<price> | <YYYY-MM-DD> | <call, $target, or "none"> | <yes / no> |

`Diverged` is yes only where the two ratings differ. A target gap inside the same
rating band is recorded in the company's `comparison.md` and is not a divergence
here.
